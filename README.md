Behold the **[Retro Scratch Forums](https://scratch.mit.edu/projects/1324228590/)** — an authentic, 1980s-style Bulletin Board System built in Scratch. I gave up my entire Memorial Day weekend, fueled purely by caffeine and retro-nostalgia, to bring you the ultimate in high-tech, low-quality browsing. 

### <img src="https://u.cubeupload.com/fmji/1f4be.png" alt="icon" height="20"> STATE-OF-THE-ART FEATURES:

* **Aggressively Retro Visuals:** Experience the REAL Scratch forums projected onto a stunning, eye-melting, glowing green retro computer screen. It’s peak aesthetic.
* **Tactical Dark Mode:** Finally, browse the forums without the blinding white light of the modern internet. Your sleep schedule will thank you.
* **The Crown Jewel of Engineering:** Prepare yourselves... **IT AUTOMATICALLY UPDATES ONCE A DAY!** That’s right. A whopping *one time* every 24 hours. Why live in the stressful, fast-paced present when you can experience the forums with a delightful, artificially engineered 24-hour delay?
